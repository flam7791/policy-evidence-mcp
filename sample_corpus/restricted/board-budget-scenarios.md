# Budget Scenarios for the Board

## Note

This fictional restricted document exists only to test that the sensitivity ceiling works. With
the ceiling at public or internal it must never be indexed or returned.

## Scenario A

Flat budget: the Institute freezes recruitment and postpones the private cloud expansion by one
year.

## Scenario B

Budget increase of four percent: the private cloud expansion goes ahead and two data engineers
are recruited.
