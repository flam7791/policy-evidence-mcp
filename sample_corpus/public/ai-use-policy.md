# Aurora Institute AI Use Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to every member of staff, consultant and intern of the Aurora Institute who
uses generative AI tools for work, whether the tool is provided by the Institute or not. It
covers text, image, code and audio generation, and AI assistants that can act on systems.

## Principles

Staff remain accountable for any output they use, whatever tool produced it. AI output is a
draft to be checked, never a final product. Tools are chosen for the task and the sensitivity of
the information involved, not for convenience. The Institute prefers approved tools with
enterprise data protection over consumer services.

## Approved tools

The Institute provides an enterprise AI assistant integrated with its document platform, and an
enterprise chat service with data retention switched off. Both are approved tools. Consumer AI
services, browser extensions and personal accounts are not approved for Institute work.

## Prohibited uses

Staff must not use AI tools to make decisions about individuals, such as recruitment,
promotion or disciplinary outcomes. Staff must not generate content that impersonates a real
person, fabricate quotations, sources or data, or upload information classified above the level
permitted for the tool.

## Human review and disclosure

A person must review AI-generated text before it is published or sent outside the Institute.
When AI generated a substantial part of a published output, the publication states it in a
short disclosure note, for example in the acknowledgements. Internal drafts do not need a
disclosure note.

## Incidents

Report suspected misuse, data exposure or harmful output to the Information Security team
within one working day. Reporting in good faith never leads to sanctions.
