# Aurora Institute Records Retention Schedule

## How to read this schedule

Each record type has a retention period, counted from the end of the calendar year in which the
record was closed, and a disposal action applied when the period ends. Records under a legal
hold are kept until the hold is lifted, whatever the schedule says.

## Governance records

Minutes and decisions of the Board are kept permanently and transferred to the archives after
ten years. Committee working papers are kept for ten years, then reviewed for historical value.

## Procurement and contracts

Signed contracts and procurement files are kept for seven years after the contract ends, then
destroyed. Unsuccessful tender submissions are kept for two years.

## Staff records

Personnel files are kept for six years after a staff member leaves. Recruitment files for
unsuccessful candidates are kept for one year.

## Email and collaboration spaces

Email is kept for three years, then deleted automatically unless it has been filed as a record.
Team sites are archived one year after their last activity and deleted after five years.

## AI prompts and outputs

Prompts and outputs from approved AI tools are not records by default. When an AI output is
used in a decision or a publication, the final version is filed with the related record and
follows that record's retention period.
