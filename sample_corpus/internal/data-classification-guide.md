# Data Classification and Handling Guide

## Levels

The Institute uses three classification levels. Public information may be shared with anyone.
Internal information is for staff and contractors and would cause limited harm if disclosed.
Restricted information would cause serious harm if disclosed, for example personal data,
negotiation positions or unpublished budget figures.

## Labelling

Every document carries a label in its header or file properties. Restricted documents must also
carry the label on every page and may only be stored in libraries with restricted access. When
in doubt, apply the higher label and ask the document owner.

## Using classified information with AI tools

Public and internal information may be used with the approved enterprise AI tools. Restricted
information must never be pasted or uploaded into any AI tool that sends data outside the
Institute's own environment, including approved external chat services. Restricted information
may only be processed by models hosted inside the Institute's private cloud, after a data
protection review.

## Sharing outside the Institute

Internal information may be shared with partners under a signed agreement. Restricted
information requires the written approval of the document owner and encrypted transfer.

## Declassification

Owners review restricted documents every two years and lower the label when the harm has
passed, for example once a budget has been published.
